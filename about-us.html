Insights & Innovation

Where Perception Meets Measurement

Much of what defines human experience—stress, effort, engagement—lives in perception. We develop scientifically validated methods to compare what people feel with what the body reveals, translating subjective experience into measurable insight that supports a wide range of human-centered applications.

Our Mission

Why We Exist

We believe every thought, decision, and effort leaves a measurable physiological signal—yet most systems still rely on what people report, not what their bodies reveal.

By objectively capturing real-time human responses, we make hidden strain visible, enabling individuals, teams, and organizations to understand limits, evaluate change, and build systems designed for long-term human sustainability.

Our Vision

Where We’re Going

We’re building a future where decisions about people—at work, in healthcare, and in high-stakes environments—are guided by objective signals, not assumptions.

By transforming subjective human states like workload, stress, and trust into measurable data, we aim to support healthier teams, more resilient organizations, and systems that adapt to humans—not the other way around.
